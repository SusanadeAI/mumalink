EPDS Clinical Protocol & Decision Support

Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale scoring rules & triage pathways.

Score 0 – 9: Normal / Stable

Healthy postpartum adjustment. Continue standard 42-day automated WhatsApp surveillance every 7 days.

Score 10 – 12: Moderate Distress

Elevated emotional distress or fatigue. Initiate daily WhatsApp check-in, alert duty midwife, and arrange clinical check-in call within 24 hours.

Score 13 – 30: Critical Risk

Severe distress / probable clinical depression. Immediate clinician notification. Urgent telephone consultation and in-person referral to specialist care.

Question 10 Flag (Self-Harm Trigger)

Any score > 0 on Question 10 ("thought of harming myself") triggers an instant emergency alert, bypassing regular queues for immediate clinical intervention.
